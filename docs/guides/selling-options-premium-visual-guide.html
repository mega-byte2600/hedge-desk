Stop signs

The fastest way to protect a premium strategy is to know when not to open it.

No naked callsLoss can be theoretically unlimited as the underlying rises.
No unfunded putsA $50 strike can create a $5,000 stock purchase per standard contract.
No event blindnessEarnings and dividends can change price and early-assignment risk.
No market orders in thin chainsA wide bid/ask can consume the premium edge before the trade begins.
