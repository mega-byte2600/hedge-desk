### Implied-volatility forecasting

1. **"Meta-Learning Neural Process for Implied Volatility Surfaces with SABR-induced Priors"** — Jirong Zhuang, Xuan Wu. arXiv:2509.11928, submitted 15 Sep 2025 (v3 Oct 2025). Relevance: single-pass IV-surface reconstruction from sparse quotes with a SABR structural prior; beats SABR/SSVI/GP baselines on SPX options, ~40% RMSE reduction vs. market-data-only ablation — directly applicable to the Overnight Premium Desk's IV-rank/vol screens. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.11928. Credibility: preprint (q-fin.CP), authors unaffiliated in the listing; methodologically concrete with reported ablations, but unreviewed.

2. **"Deep Learning Option Pricing with Market Implied Volatility Surfaces"** — Lijie Ding, Egang Lu, Kin Cheung. arXiv:2509.05911, 9 Sep 2025. Relevance: VAE compresses SPX vol surfaces (2018–2023) to 10 latent dims + MLP pricer for American/Asian options in one forward pass — candidate fast pricer for the premium desk's paper evaluations. URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.05911. Credibility: preprint (q-fin.CP); one author at Oak Ridge National Lab, one independent, one industry (Valkin Holdings). Unreviewed.

3. **"Fusing Narrative Semantics for Financial Volatility Forecasting" (M2VN)** — Yaxuan Kong, Yoontae Hwang, Marcus Kaiser, Chris Vryonides, Roel Oomen, Stefan Zohren (Oxford / Deutsche Bank). arXiv:2510.20699, 23 Oct 2025. Relevance: fuses market features with news embeddings from a *point-in-time* LLM ("Time Machine GPT") to kill look-ahead bias — the most methodologically relevant item for the Quant Lab's validation standards, and applicable to the Earnings and Futures Event desks' news-driven vol forecasting. URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.20699. Credibility: preprint (q-fin.CP) with industry co-authors from Deutsche Bank; strong on the leakage question, but unreviewed.

### Volatility risk premium / premium-selling

4. **"Sizing the Risk: Kelly, VIX, and Hybrid Approaches in Put-Writing on Index Options"** — Maciej Wysocki (Univ. of Warsaw, Quantitative Finance Research Group). arXiv:2508.16598, Aug 2025. Relevance: systematic SPXW put-writing (0–5 DTE) comparing Kelly vs. VIX-regime vs. hybrid sizing; finds ultra-short-dated far-OTM with hybrid sizing gives best risk-adjusted returns with drawdown control — the closest recent paper to the Overnight Premium Desk's actual mandate. URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.16598. Credibility: preprint (q-fin), university research group, ORCID-listed author; single-author preprint, unreviewed.

5. **"0DTEs: Trading, Gamma Risk and Volatility Propagation"** — Chukwuma Dim (GWU), Bjørn Eraker (Wisconsin), Grigory Vilkov (Frankfurt School). Working paper, 6 Jun 2025. Relevance: market-maker gamma inventory vs. intraday vol and reversal/momentum — essential risk context for any short-dated premium-selling on the Overnight Premium Desk (0DTE flow can flip intraday vol dynamics). URL: https://paperswithbacktest.com/api/paper/dtes-trading-gamma-risk-and-volatility-propagation/pdf. Credibility: working paper by established academics, presented at Cboe RMC 2023 Academic Roundtable and multiple seminars; not yet journal-published (stated in acknowledgments).

### Earnings-announcement drift

6. **"Keeping it Simple: How Can Post-Earnings Return Drift Exist and Not Exist Simultaneously?"** — Avanidhar Subrahmanyam (UCLA Anderson). Working paper, 2025 (SSRN 5930255, per UCLA Anderson Review). Relevance: shows the 2025 "PEAD is back" claims hinge on including microcaps (t-stat 2.18 with, 1.43 without) and research-design choices — directly disciplines how the Earnings Event Desk should measure drift (filter microcaps, simple methods over statistical complexity). URL: https://anderson-review.ucla.edu/is-post-earnings-announcement-drift-a-thing-again/ (UCLA review summarizing the working paper). Credibility: working paper by a chaired finance professor; the review page is a university publication, not the paper itself — the full paper sits behind SSRN.

7. **"News diffusion in social networks and stock market reactions"** — David Hirshleifer, Lin Peng, Qiguang Wang. *Review of Financial Studies* 38(3), 883–937, 2025. Relevance: drift exists with high confidence but weakens with greater social connectedness — a measurable moderator the Earnings Desk could use to condition expected drift magnitude. Credibility: peer-reviewed, top-tier finance journal (RFS) — the strongest credential in this sweep. Full text paywalled (standard for RFS); abstract and findings confirmed via the UCLA review citation.

### Dividend forecasting

8. **"Dividend Growth: Investor Expectations, and the Prediction of Dividend Growth"** — Hang Dong (Univ. of Georgia). FMA 2025 Taipei working paper, 25 Aug 2025. Relevance: state-space model extracting dividend-growth expectations from dividend futures; outperforms existing approaches on S&P 500 (and Euro Stoxx 50 / Nikkei / FTSE), especially in downturns — candidate forecasting approach for the Dividend Opportunity Desk. URL: https://www.fmaconferences.org/Taipei/Papers/206.pdf. Credibility: conference working paper (FMA), academic author; **caveat:** estimation uses proprietary OTC dividend-futures data — methodology is transferable but the dataset itself is not open, so flag as WATCH/TEST only.

9. **"Predicting the Predictable: Decomposing and Forecasting Stock Returns in a Data-rich Environment" (FA-SOP)** — Tingting Cheng (Nankai Univ.) et al., Jun 2025. Relevance: decomposes returns into dividend-price ratio + earnings growth + P/E-multiple growth and forecasts each separately with macro factors; beats standard predictive regressions out-of-sample — the dividend-yield and earnings components map directly onto the Dividend and Earnings desks' ranking problems. URL: https://www.capitalspectator.com/research-review-15-august-2025-forecasting/ (research-review summary; direct paper URL not pinned). Credibility: summarized by an independent research-review blog; underlying paper is a working paper not read in full — treat as a lead, not a verified find.

### Purged/embargoed validation methodology

10. **sp500-walkforward-harness** — open-source repo by ilyamirin, actively updated (README current as of ~Sep 2026). Relevance: implements purged K-fold CV with a 10-day embargo as the *only* cross-validator in the package (a test greps the source to enforce it), inside a walk-forward harness comparing rule-based vs. LightGBM strategies on identical windows and costs — the closest thing to a runnable reference implementation of the Quant Lab's validation standard. URL: https://github.com/ilyamirin/sp500-walkforward-harness/blob/HEAD/README.md. Credibility: open-source repo, not peer-reviewed; code is inspectable and the design choices are explicitly documented. Canonical academic basis remains López de Prado (2018) — predates the window but is the origin of purged/embargoed CV; no newer peer-reviewed superset found in this sweep.
