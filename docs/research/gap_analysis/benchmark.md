## A. Reference architecture of an institutional trading desk

Below is a US broker-dealer / buy-side reference model. Rows cover functions. A buy-side adviser outsources some of them (market access, clearing, CAT reporting) to its executing brokers. In 2026, regulation is moving in several places. The table and the notes after it flag what changed.

| Recordkeeping | WORM or audit-trail storage of records and communications | SEC 17a-4 as amended Oct 12, 2022, which allows an audit-trail alternative to WORM ([SEC 2022-187](https://www.sec.gov/news/press-release/2022-187)). | Model risk management | Inventory, independent validation, outcomes analysis, governance | **Changed 2026:** On Apr 17, 2026 the OCC, Fed, and FDIC replaced the 2011 guidance (SR 11-7 / OCC 2011-12) with risk-based guidance. | Algo trading governance | Development, testing, deployment, monitoring, and the ability to disable algos quickly | [FINRA RN 15-09](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/15-09): cross-disciplinary risk review, change management, QA separated from development, segregated test environments, post-launch alerts, limits on trader overrides, and a quick-disable mechanism | In-house SDLC and release gates |
| Customer suitability / Reg BI | Recommendation must meet care, disclosure, conflict, and compliance obligations | [FINRA 2111](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/key-topics/regulation-best-interest). [SEC Reg BI](https://www.sec.gov/tm/regulation-best-interest) (2019 final rule) | CRM/suitability engines |
| Options account approval | Approval levels, ROP sign-off, options disclosure document | [FINRA Rule 2360](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/2360?page=0). [FINRA options sweep update](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/guidance/sweep-letters-and-updates/update-option-account-opening-and-supervision) | Broker account-opening systems |
| AI-specific guidance | Governs gen-AI use in supervision, communications, and research | [FINRA RN 24-09](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/24-09) (Jun 27, 2024) says rules are technology-neutral and cover in-house and third-party gen-AI (3110, 2210, model risk, data integrity). It creates no new requirements. **SEC predictive-data-analytics proposal (Aug 2023) was WITHDRAWN on Jun 12, 2025.** Any revival must restart with a new proposal ([Proskauer](https://www.proskauer.com/alert/sec-withdraws-fourteen-rule-proposals)) | Model gateways, prompt/response logging, LLM eval suites |
| Cybersecurity / privacy | Incident response, breach notice, vendor oversight | **Reg S-P amendments** (adopted May 16, 2024): a written incident-response program, customer notice within 30 days, and service-provider notice within 72 hours ([SEC compliance guide](https://sec.gov/files/rules/final/2024/regulation-s-p-small-entity-compliance-guide.pdf)). Compliance was due Dec 3, 2025 for larger entities and Jun 3, 2026 for smaller ones ([Sidley](https://www.sidley.com/en/insights/newsupdates/2026/04/us-sec-regulation-s-p-compliance-deadline-approaching-for-smaller-entities)) | SIEM/SOAR, secrets vaults, vendor-risk platforms |

**What this means for Emporion (inference, not legal advice):** A paper-only research desk with no customers and no order routing is outside most of these rules: 15c3-5, CAT, 5310, 2360, Reg BI. The controls still serve as **design benchmarks**:
- 15c3-5-style hard pre-trade gates
- 15-09-style algo SDLC
- 17a-4-style immutable records
- MRM-style independent validation
- 24-09-style gen-AI governance

Under the 2026 interagency guidance, deterministic rule-based processes fall outside the "model" definition ([OCC 2026-13](https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2026/bulletin-2026-13.html)). That fits the desk's choice to keep Risk of Ruin in a separately versioned deterministic component.

## C. Multi-agent / swarm end-to-end trading patterns

### C1. Architectural patterns seen in the literature and OSS
1. **Role specialization modeled on a trading firm.**
   - TradingAgents: analysts, then a bull/bear researcher debate, then a trader, then risk management, then a portfolio-manager approval ([arXiv 2412.20138](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.20138); [repo](https://github.com/TauricResearch/TradingAgents)).
   - FinRobot: a lead agent with pipeline and bull/bear/judge agents ([repo](https://github.com/AI4Finance-Foundation/FinRobot)).
2. **Memory and reflection.**
   - FinMem: profiling, layered memory, and decision modules ([arXiv 2311.13743](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.13743)).
   - FinAgent: multimodal input, dual-level reflection, and diversified memory retrieval ([arXiv 2402.18485](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18485)).
3. **Tool use and computation outside the model.** FinRobot's split: the model reasons and the software computes ([repo](https://github.com/AI4Finance-Foundation/FinRobot)). The 2026 SoK below makes this a security requirement.
4. **Backtest in the loop.**
   - Man's AlphaGPT writes code and backtests it, and humans approve signals for live trading ([Hedgeweek](https://www.hedgeweek.com/man-group-deploys-agentic-ai-for-quant-signal-discovery/)).
   - RD-Agent(Q) runs a propose → implement → evaluate loop on Qlib ([repo](https://github.com/microsoft/RD-Agent)).
5. **Human-in-the-loop gates.**
   - Man: multiple checkpoints ([Hedgeweek](https://www.hedgeweek.com/man-group-deploys-agentic-ai-for-quant-signal-discovery/)).
   - Two Sigma: humans remain vigilant supervisors ([Two Sigma](https://www.twosigma.com/articles/ai-in-investment-management-2026-outlook-part-i)).
   - FINRA 15-09: QA kept separate from development and a quick-disable mechanism ([FINRA](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/15-09)).
6. **Simulation environments.** StockAgent simulates investors in a market and blocks the model from using prior knowledge of the test period ([arXiv 2407.18957](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.18957)).

### C2. Evidence on whether LLM agents work (2025–2026 benchmarks)
- **FINSABER** ([arXiv 2505.07078](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07078v5)). Covers two decades and 100+ symbols. Reported LLM timing advantages shrink substantially. The strategies are too conservative in bull markets and too aggressive in bear markets. The authors trace earlier claims to survivorship and data-snooping bias.
- **StockBench** ([arXiv 2510.02209](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02209), v2 Mar 2026). Contamination-free and multi-month. Most LLM agents fail to beat buy-and-hold. Static financial QA scores do not predict trading behavior.
- **Agent Market Arena** ([arXiv 2510.11695](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11695)). A live benchmark across crypto and stocks. Agent architecture explains more of the outcome variance than the model backbone does.
- **LiveTradeBench** ([arXiv 2511.03628](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.03628)). 50-day live runs of 21 LLMs. High LMArena scores do not imply better trading.
- **Takeaway (inference):** The published evidence does **not** support autonomous LLM alpha. It does support LLMs as research and throughput tools inside deterministic, independently validated gates. That matches Griffin's Oct 2025 view and Man's checkpoint model above.

### C3. Known failure modes (with sources)
| Failure mode | Evidence | Control implied |
|---|---|---|
| Look-ahead / memorization leakage | LLM forecast accuracy is inflated within the training window and the effect vanishes after the cutoff. The Lookahead Propensity test measures it ([arXiv 2512.23847](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23847)). TradingAgents shipped look-ahead and point-in-time fixes in 2026 ([repo](https://github.com/TauricResearch/TradingAgents)). Mitigation research: [chronologically consistent LLMs](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.21206), [DatedGPT](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.11838) | Point-in-time data. Evaluate only after the model's cutoff. Run contamination tests |
| Hallucination / inconsistent outputs | Still present in Man's production agentic research ([Hedgeweek](https://www.hedgeweek.com/man-group-deploys-agentic-ai-for-quant-signal-discovery/)) | Citation checks, deterministic recomputation, human sign-off |
| Error propagation across agents | Small perturbations in one component cascade into concentration, runaway exposure, and drawdowns ([TradeTrap, arXiv 2512.02261](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02261)) | Per-stage schema validation. Limits enforced outside the LLM |
| Prompt injection / adversarial news | One day of manipulated headlines (invisible Unicode, hidden text) over 14 months cut annual returns by up to 17.7 pp ([arXiv 2601.13082](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13082)) | Source provenance, Unicode normalization, cross-source corroboration |
| Missing out-of-model safeguards | A 2026 SoK of 15 schemes found none fully meets its robustness metrics. Of 7 audited codebases, none enforces stop-losses or position limits outside the LLM. 4 lack components they document, and 2 execute model-generated code on the host ([arXiv 2609.19705](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19705)) | Hard limits and model-bypassing interrupts (kill switch). Sandboxed code execution |
| Overfitting / multiple testing | The Deflated Sharpe Ratio corrects for selection bias across many trials and for non-normal returns ([Bailey & López de Prado 2014](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2460551)). See also Probability of Backtest Overfitting ([SSRN 2326253](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2326253)). Vectorized sweeps make thousands of trials cheap ([vectorbt](https://github.com/polakowo/vectorbt)) | Log the trial count. Report DSR/PBO |
| Non-determinism | Inference outputs vary with server load because batch size changes. Batch-invariant kernels give deterministic output ([Thinking Machines via S. Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Sep/11/defeating-nondeterminism)). TradingAgents warns its results are non-deterministic ([repo](https://github.com/TauricResearch/TradingAgents)) | Record model, version, seed, and prompt hash. Treat LLM output as untrusted input. Keep authoritative numbers in deterministic code |

### C4. Evaluation practice
- **Walk-forward / out-of-sample after model cutoff.** Contamination-free live benchmarks: StockBench, AMA, LiveTradeBench (above).
- **Purged k-fold with embargo, and combinatorial purged CV.** López de Prado introduced these ([Advances in Financial ML, Wiley 2018](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/advances-in-financial/9781119482086/b_01.xhtml)). Summaries: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purged_cross-validation) (the page is flagged for reliability), and an implementation in [skfolio CombinatorialPurgedCV](https://skfolio.org/generated/skfolio.model_selection.CombinatorialPurgedCV.html).
- **Deflated Sharpe and PBO** ([SSRN 2460551](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2460551), [SSRN 2326253](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2326253)).
- **Long horizons and broad universes** to avoid survivorship and snooping ([FINSABER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07078v5)).
- **Robustness and adversarial testing:** TradeTrap component perturbation ([arXiv 2512.02261](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02261)) and adversarial headlines ([arXiv 2601.13082](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13082)).
- **Regime-split reporting** (bull vs. bear) ([FINSABER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07078v5)).

## D. Benchmark scorecard dimensions (0–5)

Scale: 0 = absent, 1 = ad hoc, 2 = documented, 3 = credible OSS standard, 4 = audited/enforced, 5 = top-firm practice. The level-5 and level-3 anchors come from Sections A–C. For a **paper-only** desk, execution, clearing, and suitability are scored as *design readiness*, not live capability.

| # | Dimension | How to measure | Level 5 (top firm) | Level 3 (credible OSS) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Data quality, latency, and provenance | % of fields with source, as-of, and license. Staleness detection. Point-in-time correctness | Direct feeds plus SIP/OPRA with NBBO validation ([Databento OPRA](https://databento.com/microstructure/opra)). Point-in-time security master and corporate actions. Cross-source corroboration ([SoK 2609.19705](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19705)) | Free/EOD sources with a cache and a point-in-time option (TradingAgents v0.5 PIT, [repo](https://github.com/TauricResearch/TradingAgents)). Delay is labeled |
| 2 | Pre-trade risk controls and kill switch | Hard limits outside the model. Synchronous gate on every decision. Quick-disable ([RN 15-09](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/15-09)) | Pre-trade checks and stop-losses in code (FinRL-X, [repo](https://github.com/AI4Finance-Foundation/FinRL-Trading)). **Scoring guidance:**
- Score from command-verified evidence (VERIFIED), not documentation.
- A control that exists but has no production caller scores at most 2. The internal automation-patterns note found exactly this for the kill switch and `approve_paper_trade`.
- Dimensions 2, 5, 6, and 10 are where the published research shows OSS agent projects fail most ([SoK 2609.19705](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19705); [TradeTrap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02261)). They are the most likely to set Emporion apart.
